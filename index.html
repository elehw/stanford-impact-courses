Reading an empty field. A blank CSI area, theme or ecopreneurial objective means the programme that maintains that list has not assessed this course — never that the course does not qualify.

Timetables. A course marked no timetable yet is in the catalogue but Stanford has not published its 2026-27 schedule. That is not the same as cancelled — check ExploreCourses before planning around it.

Theme and Capability. Two axes, one question each. Theme says what a course is about — 11 values adapted from the MSx CSI survey verticals. Capability says what you will be able to do after it — 10 values derived from the CSI certificate's own Area concepts. Both are read from the course description and both can be empty; a pure science course teaches a subject without building a professional capability.

What is hidden by default. Courses that have not run in the last two years, and courses whose code is not in the ExploreCourses catalogue — including Stanford Online professional programmes, which are not catalogue courses at all — are hidden until you tick Also show courses not in the current catalogue at the bottom of the filters. No course was left out on a judgement about whether it is impact-related enough.
